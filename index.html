✂️ Cut the “like”

Turn each simile into a metaphor in two moves.

  1. Cut like or as, so the first thing is the second thing.
  2. Explain it. Say where it is, what it does or how it feels.

🧩 Common Ground

Two wheels, two things that seem to have nothing in common. Your job is to find what connects them.

+

Find three things they have in common.

Now pick your best connection and write the metaphor.

🪜 Level-Up Ladder

Climb four rungs with one body part. Each rung is a little more poetic.

Example with “hands”

  1. Plain: My hands are cold.
  2. Simile: My hands are cold like ice.
  3. Metaphor: My hands are ice.
  4. Extended: My hands are ice that freezes every handshake.

🔎 Detective

Is it a simile, a metaphor, or just a fact? Be careful: some sentences are tricky.

✍️ Poem Builder

Describe a person with one metaphor for each step. It can be you, a friend or a character from a story.

Go from general to specific, just like the description ladder from class: start with the whole person and zoom in until you reach the smallest special detail.
